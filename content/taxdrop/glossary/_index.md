This glossary provides definitions and explanations of key property tax terms, housing laws, and propositions that affect homeowners in California and Texas. Understanding these terms can help you navigate property tax assessments, exemptions, and appeals.

## California

- [Proposition 13](/taxdrop/glossary/california-proposition-13/) - Property tax assessment limitations
- [Proposition 19](/taxdrop/glossary/california-proposition-19/) - Property tax transfer rules for seniors and disaster victims
- [SB 79](/taxdrop/glossary/california-sb-79/) - Property tax postponement program expansion

## Texas

- [SB 2 Property Tax Reform](/taxdrop/glossary/texas-sb-2/) - Property tax revenue limitations
- [Homestead Exemption](/taxdrop/glossary/texas-homestead-exemption/) - Primary residence tax exemptions
